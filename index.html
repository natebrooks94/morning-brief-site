Frase del día
¡Qué majo!
keh MAH-hoh (for a woman: keh MAH-hah)
How kind / What a nice person

Spaniards say it about someone friendly or kind, like a helpful waiter or your friend's parents. It's warmer and more casual than 'qué amable,' and you'll hear it all the time in Spain.

Lugar del día

Chocolatería San Ginés

Chocolatería San Ginés has served churros and thick hot chocolate in the Pasadizo de San Ginés since 1894, when it opened as a churrería. It's Madrid's classic stop for chocolate con churros, especially late at night, and it's a short walk from Puerta del Sol. It doesn't take reservations, so expect to wait for a table at busy times.

Hours
Open today, with no closure reported. 24 hours Thursday to Sunday and on the eve of holidays; Monday to Wednesday 8:00 to 23:59.
Entry
Free to walk in; you pay for what you order. Prices are low. No table reservations, especially from Nov 15 through the Christmas season.
Metro
Sol (Lines 1, 2, 3) or Ópera (Lines 2, 5, Ramal). Line 1 was briefly suspended this morning and is running again.
Tip
Light rain and gusts up to 49 km/h today make it a good day to sit inside with chocolate. Tonight's Spain-England match will fill the center, so go mid-afternoon, or on dry Monday morning.
