Frase del día
¡Venga!
BEN-gah (the v is said like a soft b)
Come on! / Let's go!

Spain's all-purpose word for 'come on,' 'OK, deal' or 'let's go,' used to hurry someone up, agree to a plan or wind down a phone call ('Venga, hasta luego'). '¡Venga ya!' means 'no way!'

Estadio Santiago Bernabéu

Real Madrid's home ground is one of the most famous stadiums in football. The self-guided tour takes you through the trophy museum and to a panoramic view of the stadium. With rain and strong gusts forecast today, this mostly indoor visit is a good pick.

Nothing special is posted for today. On match days the tour closes about 5.5 hours before kickoff, and the changing rooms and benches close the day before a match. The pitch can be stored underground between games, so a ticket doesn't guarantee a view of the grass.
