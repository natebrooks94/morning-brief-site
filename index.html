Iran Conflict & Fallout

Diplomacy · Hormuz · Energy
StatusThere are no talks and no ceasefire. Trump says Washington doesn't need a deal and has taken Vance off the file. Tanker attacks in Hormuz continue while the US enforces its naval blockade, and Israel is reportedly preparing for a possible major strike.

Trump: 'We don't need a deal with Iran,' pulls Vance off talksDeveloping · Day 7

New today: Trump wrote on Truth Social that the US no longer needs a deal because "we've got their Strait," and told VP Vance to put his full attention on alleged corruption in Democratic-run states and cities. The New York Times, Washington Post and Reuters all report this as an end to Vance's negotiating role. It reverses a WSJ report from Wednesday that Trump had told Vance to restart talks right away and that Vance was weighing face-to-face talks with Iran's security chief Mohsen Rezaei. Oil rose on the post.

Tehran's position is just as hard. Rezaei said Iran won't let "a single particle" of enriched material leave the country because it needs the fuel for planned nuclear-powered ships and submarines. He also said any US base used in a new war would be a target, including bases in Europe. Basij chief Hossein Taeb said Washington must accept seven conditions before talks can start, though Tehran has never published themOpposition-leaning. On Veterans Day, Trump said the US had won but wants "a total victory, not a 93% victory."

Why it matters: With both sides refusing to talk, the lower oil prices of recent weeks depend on the blockade holding, not on any deal.

New today: Jensen Huang resigned as Nvidia's CEO, president and board member, effective immediately. Why it matters: Nvidia supplies and helps finance much of the AI industry, so a long leadership gap could slow spending across the sector.

Frase del día
No pasa nada
noh PAH-sah NAH-dah
No worries / It's fine

Spaniards use it all day to mean "no worries" or "it's fine." Say it when someone bumps you, apologizes for being late or spills a little of your caña.

Lugar del día

Barrio de las Letras

Barrio de las Letras is the old literary quarter between Sol and the Paseo del Prado, where Cervantes, Lope de Vega and Quevedo lived. Lines from their work are set in gold letters into the pavement, and Calle Huertas is full of old bars. The one indoor stop is the Casa Museo Lope de Vega, the playwright's 17th-century house, which you can see on a short free guided visit.

Hours
The streets are open all the time. Casa Museo Lope de Vega is open Tue–Sun 10 AM–6 PM (last visit 5 PM) and closed Mondays, so it's open today. Hours can change for special events.
Entry
