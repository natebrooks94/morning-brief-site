Diplomacy · Hormuz · Energy
StatusThere is no ceasefire and no talks with Washington. Attacks on shipping in Hormuz continue, and Trump is weighing a large new US-Israeli air campaign after he returns from Asia. Trump has called a preliminary deal "a waste of time."

Trump weighs a big new strike campaign as Israel pushes for 4 to 7 days of joint raidsNew

The New York Times reports that Trump is considering another large round of strikes on Iran. Netanyahu is pressing for a joint air campaign of four to seven days against missile, drone and energy targets. No decision has been made. Axios says the options include suspected nuclear sites such as Pickaxe Mountain, bridges, power plants and Iranian leaders, and that the White House has asked how Iran would retaliate against Gulf oil and gas facilities under each option.

Israeli officials told Ynet that Israel is at its highest alert while it waits for Trump's decision; CENTCOM's Adm. Brad Cooper was in Israel for rare joint planning. Reuters says Mossad chief Roman Gofman lobbied for joint strikes in Washington last week. Gulf leaders are urging Trump to tighten economic pressure instead. Trump told Fox a preliminary agreement was "a waste of time" and that he wants a lasting dealOpposition-leaning.

Why it matters: Trump's decision, likely after he returns from Asia, decides whether the war fully resumes.

teh ah-peh-TEH-seh
Do you feel like…?

"¿Te apetece un café?" means "Fancy a coffee?" and it's the everyday Spain way to suggest a plan, softer than "¿quieres…?" Answer "Me apetece mucho" if you're in, or "Hoy no me apetece" if you're not.

Teleférico and Casa de Campo

Closed: the Teleférico is shut for a full rebuild and won't reopen until the first quarter of 2027. Crews have pulled the old cables and are demolishing the stations and towers.

When it returns, the 2.5 km ride from Pintor Rosales over to Casa de Campo will take 10 to 12 minutes, in 47 new 10-person cabins, some with glass floors. For now, Casa de Campo itself is the draw: Madrid's huge former royal hunting ground, with a lake, pine woods and long views back to the Royal Palace.
