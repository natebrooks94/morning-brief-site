Frase del día
Una caña, por favor
OO-nah KAH-nyah, por fah-BOR
A small draft beer, please

A caña is a small glass of draft beer, the standard order at a Madrid bar counter. It's smaller than a pint, so people often order another round later instead of one big glass.

Lugar del día

Palacio Real

The Palacio Real is the Spanish royal family's official residence, though they don't live there. It is used for state ceremonies, and visitors tour its huge state rooms, throne room and royal armory. Today is Saturday, one of the regular days for the changing of the guard, unless official events or bad weather cancel it.

The palace can close without warning for official events, so check Patrimonio Nacional's site before you go. The Royal Kitchen needs a separate paid ticket.

Hours
Today 10:00–18:00 Madrid time, last entry 17:00. Sunday 10:00–16:00.
Entry
Buy on Patrimonio Nacional's official site. The free slots (Mon–Thu, 16:00–18:00, EU residents and Latin Americans) don't apply today.
Metro
Ópera (L2, L5, R) or Plaza de España (L2, L3, L10)
Tip
Enter from Plaza de la Armería by the Almudena Cathedral. Expect a foggy, dry 6–10°C, so wear a warm layer.
