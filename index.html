Frase del día
¿Quedamos para tomar algo?
keh-DAH-mohs PAH-rah toh-MAR AHL-goh
Shall we meet up for a drink?

It's the easy, casual way to suggest meeting up for a drink or a bite. Use it with friends or new acquaintances. 'Tomar algo' can mean a caña, a coffee or a vermut, so it never sounds like pressure.

Lugar del día

Parque del Retiro

El Retiro is Madrid's great central park: 125-odd hectares of gardens, tree-lined paseos and the big Estanque lake, watched over by the Alfonso XII monument. It's where Madrileños walk off a long lunch. A late-November visit means autumn colors and quieter paths.

The Palacio de Cristal and Palacio de Velázquez host Reina Sofía exhibitions. The city can fence off areas or close the park under its weather-alert protocol, but no alert is posted today, and today's gusts are light.
