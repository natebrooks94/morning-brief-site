Diplomacy · Hormuz · Energy
StatusActive, near-daily fighting without a declared war: the US struck Iran's Qeshm Island on Tuesday, and Iran answered with drones on US bases in Kuwait and Bahrain. Diplomacy is stalled, though Iranian media say Tehran is 'seriously pursuing' a diplomatic end.

New today: US jets struck missile and air-defense sites on Qeshm Island near Hormuz on Tuesday, a US military official said. Early reports put at least six people killed or injuredUnconfirmed. OpenAI released GPT-6.2 Astra, a frontier model for 'complex professional work,' and Sol, its everyday model. Come on! / Let's go!

You'll hear it constantly in Spain: 'come on,' 'let's go,' 'OK, deal' and even 'bye' at the end of a call ('venga, hasta luego'). Say '¡Venga ya!' for 'no way!'
