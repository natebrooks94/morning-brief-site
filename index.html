Madrid Corner

Frase del día
Un montón
oon mohn-TOHN
A lot / tons

It's the everyday way to say 'a lot' or 'tons' in Spain: 'Me gusta un montón' (I like it a lot) or 'Había un montón de gente' (there were tons of people). It's casual and works in any conversation with friends.

Lugar del día

Museo Sorolla

Closed: the painter Joaquín Sorolla's house and garden on Paseo del General Martínez Campos is still shut for renovation, with no reopening date announced. A new annex at Calle Zurbano 68 opened Oct 15 with a free exhibition running until Jan 10, 2027.

When it reopens, it's worth the trip: the studio and Andalusian-style garden are one of Madrid's calmest corners and are full of his light-soaked beach paintings. For now, the annex show is the way in.

Hours
Main house closed for renovation. It normally closes Mondays and opens Tue–Sat 9:30–20:00, Sun 10:00–15:00. Check the annex's hours before you go.
Entry
Annex exhibition free. Normal ticket €3, free Saturdays from 14:00 and Sundays.
Metro
