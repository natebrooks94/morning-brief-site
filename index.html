Frase del día
¡Buen provecho!
bwen proh-BEH-choh
Enjoy your meal!

Said to people who are eating, even strangers: when you pass a table, join one or leave. 'Que aproveche' (keh ah-proh-BEH-cheh) means the same thing, and you'll hear both in Spain.

Lugar del día

Day trip: Toledo

Toledo is a walled hilltop city near Madrid and makes an easy day trip. Its skyline is topped by the Alcázar and the huge Gothic cathedral, which is the main thing to see inside. Leave time to wander the steep lanes of the old town.

Hours
Cathedral: Mon–Sat 10 AM–6:30 PM, Sun 2–6:30 PM; last entry 30 minutes before closing. Times can change for services.
Entry
About €12 per a 2026 guide; buy on the cathedral's site.
Metro
Not on the Metro. Take a train or bus from Madrid.
Tip
Madrid has light rain and gusts to 45 km/h today. Thursday looks dry and overcast (2–12°C), so go then and wear shoes with grip for the cobbles.
