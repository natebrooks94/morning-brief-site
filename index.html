National & World

FAA grounds every US flight for about four hours; officials blame overloaded systemsNew

The FAA halted all departures nationwide Friday afternoon after communications failed at regional air traffic control centers, then lifted the stop after roughly four hours. Thousands of flights were canceled and more than 1.5 million passengers were disrupted. About 3,000 more flights were canceled Saturday, and airlines warn recovery will take days. The Tribune calls it the longest nationwide ground stop in 25 years. The National Weather Service also reported a nationwide radar and communications outage.

Officials say there's no sign of a cyberattack. CBS reports that an update to the private network linking the air traffic control system made it reboot and crash, and DHS Secretary Mullin said new technology "was overwhelming the older system." The FAA had just expanded its SMART AI congestion tool nationwide, but its developer says the outage wasn't caused by SMART. Transportation Secretary Duffy is asking Congress for $50 billion to modernize the system.

Why it matters: It exposes how fragile US air traffic control is, right before Thanksgiving travel, and starts a $50B funding fight.

Frase del día
La cuenta, por favor
lah KWEN-tah, por fah-BOR
The check, please

In Spain the waiter usually won't bring the bill until you ask, because hurrying you out is considered rude. Catch their eye and say it, or add "cuando puedas" (when you can) to sound relaxed.

Lugar del día

Cava Baja, La Latina

Cava Baja is a narrow medieval street in La Latina lined almost entirely with taverns, tapas bars and old-school restaurants. It's the classic place for a tapas crawl: a drink and a small plate at one bar, then on to the next. Locals pack it on weekend nights and again on Sunday after El Rastro.

Hours
The street is always open. Most bars serve lunch from about 1 PM and reopen around 7 or 8 PM, and many stay open past midnight on weekends. El Tempranillo at No. 38 lists 1-4 PM and 8 PM to midnight.
Entry
Free to walk. You pay by the drink and tapa.
Metro
La Latina is the nearest stop.
Tip
Go early, around 8 PM, to beat the weekend crowds and waits. Light rain is possible today and Sunday (about 1 mm), so bring a layer for the 10-14°C evening.
