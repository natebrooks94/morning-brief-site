Frase del día
Tío / tía
TEE-oh / TEE-ah
Dude / mate (between friends)

Literally "uncle" and "aunt," but between friends in Spain it means "dude" or "mate." You'll hear "¡Tío, qué fuerte!" ("Dude, that's crazy!") all the time. Keep it for friends and people your age, not strangers or older people you've just met.

Lugar del día

Malasaña

Malasaña is Madrid's old bohemian quarter, the heart of the 1980s Movida. Today it's a tight grid of vintage shops, cafés and bars just north of Gran Vía. Its center is Plaza del Dos de Mayo, named for the 1808 uprising against Napoleon's troops. Go to wander, browse and have a drink, then stay late, because the square gets loud at night.

A neighborhood, so always open. Shops open by day, bars run late.
