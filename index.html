Diplomacy · Hormuz · Energy
StatusNo major combat was reported in the window. The US naval blockade of Iran is still in place, and Tehran says it will fight it. Trump says strikes could resume after New Year if there's no deal, and the indirect talks look no closer.

Iran vows to break the blockade; Trump says ships will 'end up on the seabed'Developing · Day 17

New today: Iran says it will use 'all available means' against the US blockade, and Trump answered that tankers trying to run it would end up on the seabed. Tehran-based WANA says Iran was never preparing a tanker convoy to break the blockade, which contradicts last week's reports of a convoy at Kharg Island.

Shipping is finding workarounds. The WSJ says the UAE is shuttling oil past Iran in a risky Hormuz operation, and three ships appear to have left the strait by a new route along the Oman coast. A fake 'Iranian' tanker signal shows how hard traffic is to track. Middle East Eye reported explosions in the strait but gave no detailsUnconfirmed.

Why it matters: How far Iran pushes against the blockade decides whether Hormuz stays a standoff or turns back into a war.

Lean checkers accept a Claude-assisted proof of the Riemann HypothesisNew

On Nov 20, Anthropic mathematician Levent Alpöge released a claimed proof of the Riemann Hypothesis, made with Claude. It exists only as roughly 17 million lines of Lean code. TLDR says the Lean and Mathlib communities are confident it's correct, and coverage says several proof checkers accepted it. No human-readable write-up exists yet, and one independent check is still running.

Also: Thinking Machines released Inkling 2 Mini, a 26.4B-parameter open-weight model that leads open models under 100B on a coding benchmark. Tencent shipped Hy4, Nvidia released diffusion-based Nemotron-Labs-Sigma models, and METR says task length for frontier models doubles about every 3.6 months.

Why it matters: If the proof holds up, it would be the first major open math problem solved largely by an AI.

Frase del día
Un montón
oon mohn-TOHN
A lot / tons

Means 'a lot' or 'tons,' and Spaniards use it all day: 'Hay un montón de gente' (there are tons of people), or 'Me gusta un montón' (I like it a lot). It's casual but fine almost anywhere.

Lugar del día

Museo Sorolla

Closure first: Joaquín Sorolla's house-museum and garden in Chamberí are still closed for renovation, with no reopening date. What is open is the new annex at Calle Zurbano 68, which opened Oct 15. It holds a free exhibition, 'Tras la casa del artista', which traces the house from 1905 to today in paintings, drawings, photos and documents.

It's a good short stop for Sorolla's sunny Valencian light and the story of the house, until the garden reopens.

Hours
Annex hours not confirmed. The museum used to close Mondays, so check the official site before going today.
Entry
Free for the annex exhibition, through Jan 10
