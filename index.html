Iran Conflict & Fallout

Diplomacy · Hormuz · Energy
StatusThere is no full-scale fighting right now. The US naval blockade of Iran holds and talks are stalled. Trump says major strikes could resume after New Year if there's no deal, and attacks on ships near Hormuz are at their highest level of the war.

Iran vows 'all available means' against the blockade as ship attacks hit a wartime highDeveloping · Day 17

New today: Iran says it will use 'all available means' to counter the US naval blockade. That came after Trump warned Tehran not to test it, and Iran also threatened to respond to any US attack. Data reported Monday show attacks on ships near the Strait of Hormuz are at their highest level since the war began, with October and November running above every earlier month.

Safety4sea reports three tankers were struck in the strait, plus another incident in the Gulf of Oman. Some traffic is finding ways around. Three ships appear to have left Hormuz by a new route along the Oman coast, and the UAE is stepping up efforts to move its oil through the strait. Iran has been preparing a tanker convoy at Kharg Island to challenge the blockade.

Why it matters: A deliberate test of the blockade is the most likely trigger for fighting to resume.

Nvidia reinstates Jensen Huang after 'input' from senior US officialsDeveloping · Day 11

New today: Nvidia's board voted unanimously to bring back Huang as CEO, president and director, effective immediately. The shares jumped about 30%, roughly $900 billion in value. The board said it received input from 'senior levels' of the US government and is confident Huang poses no national security risk. Six days ago, board chair Harvey Jones said there were no plans to reinstate him. Trump praised the decision and said the attorney general found no evidence of unlawful conduct.

The risks remain. The company's own investigation is still going. The NYT reports Huang's former partner Alice Huang was in touch with a Chinese Ministry of State Security officer from spring 2025 until this fall, and other reports describe abusive treatment of executives. Reuters says the SEC is investigating Nvidia's accounting. Nvidia formed a national security and export control committee and is hiring a compliance chief.

Huang faces a closed-door House interview next week and later a public Senate Banking hearing. The Washington Post reports he lobbied Trump hard on China chip sales, calling China a $50 billion-a-year market. Arm's Rene Haas, whom a recruiter had approached about the Nvidia job, extended his contract through 2033.

Why it matters: The White House effectively picked Nvidia's CEO, and the probes that could reverse it are still open.

Frase del día
Un montón
oon mohn-TOHN
A lot / tons

The casual everyday way to say 'a lot' or 'tons.' It works for amounts ('un montón de gente,' tons of people) and on its own for feelings ('me gusta un montón,' I love it). Friendly and informal, and you'll hear it constantly in Madrid.

Lugar del día

Museo Sorolla

Closed today: like most Madrid museums, the Museo Sorolla is shut on Mondays. The bigger catch is that the painter's historic house and the garden in the photo are still under renovation, and the director says it will be years before visitors can return.

What you can see is the new annex at Calle Zurbano 68 in Chamberí, which reopened on Oct 15. It holds a free show, Tras la casa del artista, on the house's architecture from 1905 to 2026, running through Jan 10. It's a short visit, best paired with a walk around Chamberí.
