Frase del día
¡Vale!
BAH-leh
OK / Sounds good

Spain's all-purpose "OK": use it to agree to a plan, confirm you understood, or close a chat ("Vale, nos vemos a las ocho"). A quick, flat "vale, vale" can also mean "got it, enough."

Lugar del día

Museo del Prado

The Prado is Spain's national art museum, on the Paseo del Prado near the Retiro. It holds the great Spanish old masters, so it's the one museum to see if you only see one. It's open today, and the free last two hours draw a crowd, so go early afternoon with a paid ticket or arrive right at 6 PM.

Hours
Today (Fri) 10 AM–8 PM, last entry 7:30 PM. Sunday 10 AM–7 PM.
Entry
Paid ticket during the day; free 6–8 PM Monday to Saturday and 5–7 PM Sunday.
Metro
Banco de España (Line 2), about 650 m, or Estación del Arte (Line 1), about 800 m.
Tip
Dry and sunny-ish today, 2–12°C, so walk the Paseo del Prado. Check Metro status first: Lines 3, 7 and 9 had early-morning breakdowns.
